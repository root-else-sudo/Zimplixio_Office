# LinkedIn Post Best Practices

## The Anatomy of a High-Performing Post

Every successful LinkedIn post has three parts:

1. **Hook** — the first 2-3 lines visible before "see more." This is the most critical part. If it doesn't create curiosity or tension, the rest of the post doesn't exist.
2. **Body** — your story, insight, or lesson. Aim for 3-7 short paragraphs with plenty of white space.
3. **CTA (Call to Action)** — one clear question or prompt that invites a real reply, not just a like.

### Example Structure
> *I turned down a $500K offer. Here's why it was the best decision of my career.*
>
> Three years ago I was broke, burnt out, and building something nobody wanted. Then a big company came knocking...
>
> [Body: story / insight / lesson]
>
> What would you have done? Drop it below 👇

---

## Hook Formulas That Work

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Confession | "I spent 10 years doing X wrong." |
| Pattern interrupt | "Nobody talks about the hardest part of [topic]." |
| Provocation | "Hot take: [contrarian opinion]." |
| List tease | "5 things I wish I knew before [milestone]." |
| Story promise | "We [achieved result]. Here's exactly how." |

---

## Format: What Gets Reach

| Format | Performance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Native text posts | ⭐ Best reach | Personal stories, 3-7 short paragraphs. White space is your friend. |
| Carousels / PDFs | ⭐ High saves | Educational content, frameworks, checklists. Great for repurposing ideas. |
| Short videos (< 60s) | ✅ Good engagement | Talking head or screen share. Always add captions. |
| Posts with external links | ⚠️ Reduced reach | Move links to the first comment instead of the post body. |

---

## Dos and Don'ts

### Do
- Write like you talk — first person, direct, conversational tone.
- Use 1-2 sentence paragraphs. Long blocks kill mobile readability.
- End with a question that invites a real reply.
- Reply to every comment within the first hour after posting.
- Post 3-5x per week, consistently. Consistency beats virality.
- Use 2-3 relevant hashtags max.

### Don't
- Open with "I'm excited to share..." — it buries your hook.
- Stuff 5+ hashtags at the bottom.
- Post and ghost. Early engagement signals boost your distribution.
- Cross-post from Twitter/X verbatim — the tone and format are different.
- Put external links in the post body — move them to the first comment.

---

## Timing

- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best time window:** 8–10am in your audience's timezone
- **Reply fast:** Engage with comments within the first 60 minutes — it signals to the algorithm that your content is worth distributing.

---

## Content That Consistently Performs

- Personal lessons learned
- Contrarian takes
- Behind-the-scenes stories
- Frameworks and mental models
- Failure stories (with the lesson)
- Industry data + your point of view
- Career pivots
- Step-by-step how-tos

---

## The Core Principle

LinkedIn rewards **authenticity and specificity**. Broad inspiration content is everywhere. What stands out is *your* specific experience, your numbers, your exact lesson — told like a real person, not a press release.

> "I built a $1M business in 18 months and here's the one mistake I almost made" will always outperform "Growth requires discomfort."

**Consistency is the real secret.** Most people who get traction on LinkedIn simply posted more often, more consistently, and for longer than everyone else. Viral moments are unpredictable — a regular publishing rhythm is not.
